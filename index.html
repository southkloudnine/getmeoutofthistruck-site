Why this exists

Two of my best friends drive for a living. One runs flatbed. The other pulls a reefer, and hauled cars before that.

I hear it all. The snow, the wind, the black ice, the rain that won't quit. The warehouse that “never got” the appointment. Waiting to get loaded. Waiting to get unloaded. Waiting.

But that's not the part that sticks. What sticks are the stories: a sunrise breaking over the mountains, a sunset that lights up the plains, an ocean that shows up out of nowhere. Lakes, forests, deserts that go on forever. Their photos are unreal.

Here's the thing about layovers: spend them in the cab and they're brutal. Step out into a city you don't know, and that's where the good stuff starts.

That's what this app is for. It won't plan your life. It won't fix the warehouse. But it will get you out of that truck, and point you somewhere worth going.

The rest is up to you.

— Dima, Seattle, WA
